Stock markets

Indices by region

The bar under each price runs from today's low to today's high; the dot is the latest price and the tick is yesterday's close. Open and closed follow each exchange's regular weekday hours, shown in IST.

Interest rates

The price of money

Central banks set short-term policy rates. Bond markets set longer-term yields. Together they decide what loans, deposits and mortgages cost.

Policy rates

Set by central banks at scheduled meetings.

In India, the standing deposit facility rate is the floor and the marginal standing facility rate the ceiling for overnight interest rates, with the repo rate in between. CRR and SLR are the shares of deposits banks must keep as cash with the RBI and in safe assets such as government bonds.

Government bond yields

Live. Changes in basis points: 1 bp is 0.01 percentage point.

A yield is the return a bond pays if bought at today's price. Yields and bond prices move in opposite directions. The U.S. 10-year is the world's benchmark for long-term borrowing costs; India's 10-year plays the same role for loans in rupees.
